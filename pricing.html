FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a credit card to start?
No. The Free plan is completely free with no card required. You only need a payment method when you choose to upgrade to Starter or Business.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from your account settings at any time. You'll keep access to your paid plan until the end of the billing period, then roll back to Free automatically.
What happens to my data if I downgrade?
Your data stays intact. If you exceed Free plan limits, your existing data is preserved. You just won't be able to add new entries until you upgrade again.
Is there an annual discount?
Annual billing with a discount is on our roadmap. Sign up now and you'll be notified when it's available and grandfathered in at the best rate.
Does PODTrackerPRO connect directly to my POD platforms?
Currently PODTrackerPRO is a manual-entry and CSV-import powered tracker optimized for POD workflows. Direct API integrations are on the roadmap for a future release.
Not sure which plan is right for me?
Start with Free and no commitment. If you're actively uploading designs across multiple platforms, Starter is the sweet spot. Business is for sellers running a full-time POD operation.
