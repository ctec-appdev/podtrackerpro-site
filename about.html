The PODTrackerPRO Story

I quit corporate - product management at a healthcare EMR company - because the system was grinding me down. With four kids, a spouse, and a full house depending on me, I tried everything: affiliate marketing, dropshipping, side hustles. Nothing stuck.

Then I found print-on-demand.

My graphic design degree and screenprinting background finally had a home. Research -> validation -> design -> production. A system I could understand and improve. Sales started coming in - small, but real.

The bottleneck wasn't design. It was research.

I was spending weeks manually combing Google Trends, platform searches, and reviews just to validate a niche. Meanwhile, AI was flooding POD with generated images - lazy, soulless designs that weren't selling. The designs that did sell were made by humans who understood their audience. Designs with emotional truth.

That's the distinction that built PODTrackerPRO: AI accelerates research. Humans create meaning.

I built it for myself first. It cut my research time in half. Then I talked to other creators and heard the same frustration everywhere - endless manual research, outdated tools, software built by people who've never uploaded a design.

So I built it for everyone.

PODTrackerPRO isn't here to generate designs for you. It's here to give you the research edge so you can focus on the creative work only you can do.

Build the brand only you can build.
